# Launch fact sheet: approved figures and guardrails

**Source of truth:** *Board Technology & Innovation Governance Benchmark, Research summary, board edition*, Edition 1 draft, 22 November 2026 (build 10c), and the Press Ranger release images of 24 November 2026. The executive summary's figures win everywhere. If the edition is revised before launch, re-check every line below.

Universe: the 1,000 companies on Fortune's 2026 list ("the Fortune 1000"; "the 1,000" on second mention). Filings read from 2011 to 2026. Authors: Ashis Bhattacharya (Bhattacharya Consulting Group) and James Chase Baldwin (Intercedent Asia). Site: boardtechbenchmark.com.

## Grades (say which one applies)

- **Measured:** figures 1 to 11, 13 and 14.
- **Not established:** figure 12. Never phrase it as "committees don't work".
- **Preliminary:** any Russell 3000 figure (for example 9.8% of 1,554 Russell boards reach level 3). Label "preliminary" every time, never pool with the Fortune 1000, and keep out of hooks and bios.

## Guardrails (from the brand voice guide)

1. **No company named next to a score, ever.** No screenshots of company reads. Aggregates only.
2. **Base in the sentence.** "810 of the 1,000", "of the 957 scored mandates". Shares to one decimal place.
3. **No causal language.** Not "drives", "causes", "proves", "impact of". Use "where a committee exists, the mandate reaches strategy".
4. **The finding, then its limit.** It measures disclosed commitments, not boardroom behaviour, and every score is a floor.
5. **British spelling, no em dashes, sentence case.** "cyber security" as two words in running text.
6. **Avoid:** hazard (use risk), audited or accuracy (use "re-read by hand, agreement"), rating or ranking, tech committee (use dedicated technology committee), leverage, unlock, game-changer, failing, behind, wake-up call. No urgency or scarcity in calls to action.
7. **Calls to action:** "Read the research", "Request the full report", "Book a conversation", "Join the quarterly update".
